## 1. Core Principles of Customer Communication
All customer-facing communications generated by AI assistance and reviewed by Relationship Managers must adhere to the highest fiduciary and regulatory standards:
1. **Factual Grounding:** Every statement regarding portfolio changes, yields, or events must reference real, verifiable account data.
2. **Customer Privacy:** Personal financial data, account numbers, and passwords must never be exposed in unencrypted channels or plain email body.
3. **Clarity & Transparency:** Information must be presented in clear, plain language appropriate for the client’s segment.
4. **No Coercion or Pressure:** Communication must invite discussion and strategic review, never forcing an immediate commitment.

---

## 2. Strictly Prohibited Statements & Language

The following expressions, promises, and phrasing are **strictly prohibited** in any written or verbal outreach:
1. **Guaranteed Returns Prohibited:**
   - ❌ *"This fund guarantees an 8.5% return."*
   - ❌ *"You are certain to make money with zero risk."*
   - ❌ *"Risk-free growth opportunity."*
   - *(Note: Capital guarantees on government-backed Fixed Deposits may be stated solely within statutory insurance limits).*
2. **Artificial Urgency & Pressure Prohibited:**
   - ❌ *"Act before 5 PM today or you will miss out."*
   - ❌ *"Once-in-a-lifetime deal."*
   - ❌ *"Limited spots remaining."*
3. **Misleading Tax or Legal Advice:**
   - ❌ *"This product completely eliminates your tax obligations."*
   - Always include the caveat: *"Please consult your independent tax advisor."*
4. **Competitor Disparagement:**
   - ❌ *"Our rates beat inferior competitor bank X."*

## 3. Communication Channel Standards

### 3.1 Email Outreach
- **Subject Line:** Professional, non-sensationalized, referencing client portfolio stewardship.
- **Salutation:** Personalized with client title and name.
- **Structure:**
  1. Warm professional greeting.
  2. Clear context (e.g., *"As part of our ongoing review of your Premier portfolio, we noted that your 12-month deposit is scheduled to mature on [Date]..."*).
  3. Grounded proposal aligned with client risk profile.
  4. Non-prescriptive call-to-action (e.g., *"Would you be available for a brief 10-minute discussion this Thursday to review reinvestment alternatives?"*).
  5. Mandatory regulatory disclaimer.

### 3.2 Phone Call Scripts
- Phone scripts generated for RM preparation must provide:
  - Concise bullet points for discussion.
  - Verification questions to confirm client identity before discussing numbers.
  - Clear talking points on the proposed solution's benefits and suitability.

### 3.3 Meeting Briefs
- Formatted as executive briefing notes for face-to-face or virtual consultations.
- Summarize triggering signals, current holdings, proposed action, and expected risk/reward profile.

## 4. Mandatory Regulatory Disclosures
Every client-facing email or written letter must append the following institutional disclosure:

> **IMPORTANT REGULATORY DISCLOSURE:**  
> This communication is prepared by your dedicated Relationship Manager for informational and financial planning discussions only. It does not constitute a formal contract or commitment to lend or invest. Past performance is not indicative of future results. Mutual funds and structured products are subject to market risks, including the possible loss of principal. Terms and rates are indicative and subject to prevailing market conditions upon formal execution.

---

## 5. Human-in-the-Loop Governance Protocol
1. **No Automatic Dispatch:** Under no circumstances should an AI-generated draft be automatically transmitted to a client via email, SMS, or messaging apps.
2. **Mandatory RM Review:** The Relationship Manager must read, verify accuracy against current customer conversations, edit where necessary, and explicitly approve the draft.
3. **Manual Triggering:** Outbound communication must be initiated through standard client applications via `mailto:` or `tel:` protocols.
